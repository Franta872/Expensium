# AMO Submission Checklist

1. Sign in to Firefox Add-ons Developer Hub.
2. Choose **Submit a New Add-on** and **On this site**.
3. Upload the ZIP produced by `python3 scripts/package.py`.
4. Data collection: **none**.
5. License: **MIT**.
6. Use `amo/LISTING.md` for listing copy.
7. Paste `amo/REVIEW_NOTES.md` into reviewer notes.
8. Export the SVG mockups in `amo/assets/` to PNG/JPEG for store screenshots.
9. Submit for review.

The repository deliberately discloses that Expensium is 100% vibe-coded and AI-generated. The installed extension itself neither uses AI nor transmits data.
